Lesson 5 · 15 min

Stable × draft: reading the spec, Chrome Status, and the browsers' positions, with dates

WebMCP has already changed its name once and may change again before the origin trial ends. Whoever teaches or implements it needs to know, with dates, what the spec says, which browser runs it, and who is against it. In this lesson you read all of that straight from the primary sources with a script, and you learn to separate what is foundation from what is lab work.

02 · Where to look and what to look for

Four sources, one question each.

1. Spec

At the top of webmachinelearning.github.io/webmcp: the document type and the date. If the date has changed since your last read, reread what you use.

Look for the name of the surface. On 10/16/2026 it is document.modelContext; navigator.modelContext no longer appears.

2. Implementation

The implementation-status.md in the spec repository says where there is an origin trial (Chrome 149, Edge 150) and what else exists (Brave Leo experimental, ChatGPT Desktop).

Chrome Status (entry 5117755740913664) gives the trial range and the stage.

3. Positions

Mozilla and WebKit publish positions in open issues: mozilla/standards-positions #1412 and WebKit/standards-positions #670. The "position:" label gives the position.

4. Note it with a date

Everything that depends on a flag, origin trial, or explainer gets the "experimental on DD/MM/YYYY" stamp. WebMCP is preparation, not ranking: visibility in AI is a topic for the inemaSEO Program.

A real case of an API that changedThe switch from navigator.modelContext to document.modelContext was accepted on 05/19/2026 (PR #177). Courses written before that date teach the old API.

03 · The state of WebMCP, read from the sources

estado.sh downloads the spec and implementation-status.md, and reads the two position issues through the public GitHub API, with no login and no key. All you need is curl.

The 10/16/2026 spec has 16 mentions of document.modelContext in its text and none of navigator.modelContext. There is an origin trial in Chrome 149 and Edge 150; Mozilla is neutral, WebKit is opposed.

Old posts and courses cite the 09/17/2026 draft and still show navigator.modelContext. The date that counts is the one in the spec header on the day you read it. If estado.sh shows a date other than 10/16/2026, the spec changed after this course was written: reread it before using any example from here.
