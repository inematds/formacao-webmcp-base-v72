    03 · Your site's plan and the checker
    Seven sections, one for each topic in the course. conferir-plano.py flags a section that is missing, empty, or has a leftover <…>, and it requires at least one date. The kit includes the filled-in plan for the example shop.
    File · kit/plano-modelo.md
    # Plano do meu site para agentes

## Site e data
Site: <your site's address>
Data do diagnóstico: <dd/mm/aaaa>

## Como os agentes usam o site hoje
<output of tres-jeitos.mjs on the most important page: what worked for reading, operating, and calling>

## O que o agente enxerga
Problemas do arvore.mjs antes: <number> · depois das correções: <number>
<the fixes made: labels, a real button, main, h1, lang, alt>

## Quem pode entrar
<output of robots.py: search, user action, and training, and the decision noted down>

## Readiness
Nota: <score> em <dd/mm/aaaa> (diagnosis, not a ranking)
<the three failures or warnings I will handle first and why>

## WebMCP
Estado lido em <dd/mm/aaaa>: spec de <date>, <browsers with an origin trial>
Decisão: <wait | lab in course 2 | I already register tools with detection and fallback>

## Próximos passos
<the next course in the program and the date when I will review this plan>
